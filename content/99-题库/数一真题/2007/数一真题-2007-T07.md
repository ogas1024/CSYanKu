## 题干

7. 设向量组 $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3}$ 线性无关，则下列向量组线性相关的是（ ）

A. $\alpha_{1}-\alpha_{2},\alpha_{2}-\alpha_{3},\alpha_{3}-\alpha_{1}$.

B. $\alpha_{1}+\alpha_{2},\alpha_{2}+\alpha_{3},\alpha_{3}+\alpha_{1}$.

C. $\alpha_{1}-2\alpha_{2},\alpha_{2}-2\alpha_{3},\alpha_{3}-2\alpha_{1}$.

D. $\alpha_{1}+2\alpha_{2},\alpha_{2}+2\alpha_{3},\alpha_{3}+2\alpha_{1}$.

> [!note]- 原题截图
> ![[数一真题-2007-T07.png|500]]

> [!success]- 答案与解析
> **答案：** A
>
> **解析：** 三个向量 $(\alpha_1-\alpha_2)+(\alpha_2-\alpha_3)+(\alpha_3-\alpha_1)=0$，故 A 组相关；其余系数矩阵行列式非零，选 A。
