## 题干

4. 设函数 $f(x, y)$ 连续，则 $\int_{-2}^{2} \mathrm{d}x \int_{4-x^{2}}^{4} f(x, y) \mathrm{d}y = (\quad)$

A. $\int_{0}^{4} \left[ \int_{-2}^{-\sqrt{4-y}} f(x, y) \mathrm{d}x + \int_{\sqrt{4-y}}^{2} f(x, y) \mathrm{d}x \right] \mathrm{d}y$

B. $\int_{0}^{4} \left[ \int_{-2}^{\sqrt{4-y}} f(x, y) \mathrm{d}x + \int_{\sqrt{4-y}}^{2} f(x, y) \mathrm{d}x \right] \mathrm{d}y$

C. $\int_{0}^{4} \left[ \int_{-2}^{-\sqrt{4-y}} f(x, y) \mathrm{d}x + \int_{2}^{\sqrt{4-y}} f(x, y) \mathrm{d}x \right] \mathrm{d}y$

D. $2 \int_{0}^{4} \mathrm{d}y \int_{\sqrt{4-y}}^{2} f(x, y) \mathrm{d}x$

> [!note]- 原题截图
> ![[数一真题 2025 T04.webp|500]]

> [!success]- 答案与解析
> **答案：** A
>
> **解析：** 区域为 $0\le y\le4$ 且 $|x|\ge\sqrt{4-y},|x|\le2$，横截面分左右两段，恰为 A。D 只有在 f 关于 x 为偶函数时才可合并。
