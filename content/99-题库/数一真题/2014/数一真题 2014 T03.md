## 题干

3. 设 $f(x,y)$ 是连续函数，则 $\int_{0}^{1} \mathrm{d}y \int_{-\sqrt{1-y^{2}}}^{1-y} f(x,y) \mathrm{d}x = (\quad)$

A. $\int_{0}^{1} \mathrm{d}x \int_{0}^{x-1} f(x,y) \mathrm{d}y + \int_{-1}^{0} \mathrm{d}x \int_{0}^{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}} f(x,y) \mathrm{d}y$.

B. $\int_{0}^{1} \mathrm{d}x \int_{0}^{1-x} f(x,y) \mathrm{d}y + \int_{-1}^{0} \mathrm{d}x \int_{-\sqrt{1-x^{2}}}^{0} f(x,y) \mathrm{d}y$.

C. $\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \mathrm{d}\theta \int_{0}^{\frac{1}{\cos\theta+\sin\theta}} f(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta) \mathrm{d}r + \int_{\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\pi} \mathrm{d}\theta \int_{0}^{1} f(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta) \mathrm{d}r$.

D. $\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \mathrm{d}\theta \int_{0}^{\frac{1}{\cos\theta+\sin\theta}} f(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta) r \mathrm{d}r + \int_{\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\pi} \mathrm{d}\theta \int_{0}^{1} f(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta) r \mathrm{d}r$.

> [!note]- 原题截图
> ![[数一真题 2014 T03.png|500]]

> [!success]- 答案与解析
> **答案：** D
>
> **解析：** 区域右半部由 $x+y\le1$ 限制，左半部为单位上半圆。极坐标下分别是 $0\le\theta\le\pi/2, r\le1/(\cos\theta+\sin\theta)$ 与 $\pi/2\le\theta\le\pi,r\le1$，并含 Jacobian r，选 D。
