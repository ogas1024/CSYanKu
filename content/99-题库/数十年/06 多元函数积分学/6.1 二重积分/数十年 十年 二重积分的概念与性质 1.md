A.  $2\lim_{n\to\infty}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\sum_{j=n+1-i}^{n}f\left(\frac{i}{n},\frac{j}{n}\right)\frac{1}{n^{2}}.$

B.  $\frac{1}{2}\lim_{n\to\infty}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\sum_{j=1}^{n}f\left(\frac{i}{n},\frac{j}{n}\right)\frac{1}{n^{2}}.$
