## 题干

1.  (15-3) 设  $D=\left\{(x,y)\mid x^{2}+y^{2}\leqslant2x,x^{2}+y^{2}\leqslant2y\right\}$，函数  $f(x,y)$ 在 D 上连续，则  $\iint_{D}f(x,y)\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y=(\quad)$

A.  $\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}d\theta\int_{0}^{2\cos\theta}f(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta)rdr+\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}d\theta\int_{0}^{2\sin\theta}f(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta)rdr.$

B.  $\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}d\theta\int_{0}^{2\sin\theta}f(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta)rdr+\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}d\theta\int_{0}^{2\cos\theta}f(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta)rdr.$

C.  $2\int_{0}^{1}dx\int_{1-\sqrt{1-x^{2}}}^{x}f(x,y)dy.$

D.  $2\int_{0}^{1}dx\int_{x}^{\sqrt{2x-x^{2}}}f(x,y)dy.$
