## 题干

14. (91-4) 试证明 $n$ 维列向量 $\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\cdots,\alpha_n$ 线性无关的充分必要条件是

$$
D=\begin{vmatrix}\alpha_{1}^{T}\alpha_{1}&\alpha_{1}^{T}\alpha_{2}&\cdots&\alpha_{1}^{T}\alpha_{n}\\\alpha_{2}^{T}\alpha_{1}&\alpha_{2}^{T}\alpha_{2}&\cdots&\alpha_{2}^{T}\alpha_{n}\\\vdots&\vdots&&\vdots\\\alpha_{n}^{T}\alpha_{1}&\alpha_{n}^{T}\alpha_{2}&\cdots&\alpha_{n}^{T}\alpha_{n}\end{vmatrix}\neq0.
$$

其中 $\alpha_i^T$ 是 $\alpha_i$ 的转置，$i=1,2,\cdots,n$。
