## 题干

7. 设  $\alpha_{1}=\left(\begin{array}{l}0\\0\\c_{1}\end{array}\right)$， $\alpha_{2}=\left(\begin{array}{l}0\\1\\c_{2}\end{array}\right)$， $\alpha_{3}=\left(\begin{array}{l}1\\-1\\c_{3}\end{array}\right)$， $\alpha_{4}=\left(\begin{array}{l}-1\\1\\c_{4}\end{array}\right)$，其中  $c_{1},c_{2},c_{3},c_{4}$ 为任意常数，则下列向量组线性相关的为（）

A. $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3}$.

B. $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{4}$.

C. $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{3}, \alpha_{4}$.

D. $\alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3}, \alpha_{4}$.

> [!note]- 原题截图（OCR 校对用）
> ![[数二真题-2012-T07.png|500]]

> [!success]- 答案与解析
> **答案：** C
>
> **解析：** $\alpha_1,\alpha_3,\alpha_4$ 的前两个分量中后两列互为相反数，相应 3 阶行列式恒为 0，选 C。
