## 题干

【4】积分 I =  $\int_{0}^{2} \mathrm{d}x \int_{0}^{\frac{x^{2}}{2}} f(x, y) \mathrm{d}y + \int_{2}^{2\sqrt{2}} \mathrm{d}x \int_{0}^{\sqrt{8-x^{2}}} f(x, y) \mathrm{d}y = (\quad)$.

A.  $\int_{0}^{\sqrt{2}} dy \int_{\sqrt{y}}^{\sqrt{8-y^{2}}} f(x, y) dx$

B.  $\int_{0}^{\sqrt{2}} dy \int_{\sqrt{2y}}^{\sqrt{8-y^{2}}} f(x, y) dx$

C.  $\int_{0}^{2} dy \int_{\sqrt{2y}}^{\sqrt{8-y^{2}}} f(x, y) dx$

D.  $\int_{0}^{2} dy \int_{\sqrt{y}}^{\sqrt{8-y^{2}}} f(x, y) dx$

> ![[数二880-高数-C05基选-004.png]]

> [!success]- 答案与解析
> **答案：** C
>
> **解析：** 把原来的两块累次积分区域合并后按 y 先积分改写，边界为 $x=\sqrt{2y}$ 到 $x=\sqrt{8-y^2}$，$y\in[0,2]$，选 C。
