## 题干

【3】设向量组  $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3}, \alpha_{4}$ 线性无关，则下列向量组线性无关的是（）.

A.  $\alpha_{1} + \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{2} + \alpha_{3}, \alpha_{3} + \alpha_{4}, \alpha_{4} + \alpha_{1}$ B.  $\alpha_{1} + \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{2} + \alpha_{3}, \alpha_{3} + \alpha_{4}, \alpha_{4} - \alpha_{1}$ C.  $\alpha_{1} + \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{2} - \alpha_{3}, \alpha_{3} + \alpha_{4}, \alpha_{4} - \alpha_{1}$ D.  $\alpha_{1} - \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{2} - \alpha_{3}, \alpha_{3} - \alpha_{4}, \alpha_{4} - \alpha_{1}$

> [!note]- 原题截图（OCR 校对用）
> ![[数二880 线代 向量 基础 选择 03.webp]]

> [!success]- 答案与解析
> **答案：** B
>
> **解析：** 记 $\beta_1=\alpha_1+\alpha_2,\beta_2=\alpha_2+\alpha_3,\beta_3=\alpha_3+\alpha_4,\beta_4=\alpha_4-\alpha_1$，则 $(\beta_1,\beta_2,\beta_3,\beta_4)=(\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3,\alpha_4)C$，其中 $|C|=2\neq0$。由 $\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3,\alpha_4$ 线性无关及 $C$ 可逆知 $\beta_1,\beta_2,\beta_3,\beta_4$ 线性无关，对应选项 B；选项 A 中四个向量之和为零向量线性组合，故线性相关，可先排除。
