## 题干

【8】设向量组  $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{1} - 2\alpha_{2} + \alpha_{3}$ 线性无关，则下列向量组线性无关的是（）.

A.  $\alpha_{1} + \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{2} + \alpha_{3}, \alpha_{3} - \alpha_{1}$ B.  $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3}$ C.  $\alpha_{1} - \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{2} - \alpha_{3}, \alpha_{1} - 2\alpha_{2} + \alpha_{3}$ D.  $\alpha_{1} + \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{2} + \alpha_{3}, \alpha_{1} + 2\alpha_{2} + \alpha_{3}$

> [!note]- 原题截图（OCR 校对用）
> ![[数二880-线代-C09基选-008.png]]

> [!success]- 答案与解析
> **答案：** B
>
> **解析：** 依题意 $\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3$ 与 $\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_1-2\alpha_2+\alpha_3$ 可互相线性表示，故二者的秩相同；已知后者线性无关，秩为 3，从而 $\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3$ 也线性无关，选 B。
