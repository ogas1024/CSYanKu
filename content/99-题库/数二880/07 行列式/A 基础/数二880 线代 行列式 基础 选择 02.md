## 题干

【2】设  $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3}, \beta_{1}, \beta_{2}$ 均是 4 维列向量，且 4 阶行列式  $\left|\left(\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{1}, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_{2}, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_{3}, \boldsymbol{\beta}_{1}\right)\right| = \mathrm{a}, \left|\left(\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{1}, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_{2}, \boldsymbol{\beta}_{2}, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_{3}\right)\right| = \mathrm{b}$，则行列式  $\left|\left(\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{3}, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_{2}, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_{1}, \boldsymbol{\beta}_{1} + \boldsymbol{\beta}_{2}\right)\right| = (\quad)$.

A.  $a + b$ B. a - b C. b - a D.  $-(a + b)$

> [!note]- 原题截图（OCR 校对用）
> ![[数二880 线代 行列式 基础 选择 02.webp]]

> [!success]- 答案与解析
> **答案：** C
>
> **解析：** 由行列式按列可加性，$|\alpha_3,\alpha_2,\alpha_1,\beta_1+\beta_2|=|\alpha_3,\alpha_2,\alpha_1,\beta_1|+|\alpha_3,\alpha_2,\alpha_1,\beta_2|$；交换第1、3列各得 $-|\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3,\beta_1|=-a$ 与 $-|\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3,\beta_2|$，而 $|\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3,\beta_2|=-|\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\beta_2,\alpha_3|=-b$，故原式 $=-a-(-b)=b-a$，选 C。
