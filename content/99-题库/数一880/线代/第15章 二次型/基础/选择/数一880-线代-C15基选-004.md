## 题干

【4】二次型  $f(x_{1},x_{2},x_{3})=x_{1}^{2}+4x_{2}^{2}+4x_{3}^{2}-4x_{1}x_{2}+4x_{1}x_{3}-8x_{2}x_{3}$ 的规范形为（）.

A. $f = z_{1}^{2}$

B. $f = z_{1}^{2} - z_{2}^{2}$

C. $f = z_{1}^{2} + z_{2}^{2} + z_{3}^{2}$

D. $f = z_{1}^{2} + z_{2}^{2} - z_{3}^{2}$
