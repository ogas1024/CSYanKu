## 题干

15. 下列代码的时间复杂度为（ ）

```c
for (int i = 1; i <= n; i += 2 )
    for (int j = 1; j <= n; j *= 2)
        for (int k = 1; k <= j; k ++ )
            count ++ ;
```

A. $O(n^2\log_2 n)$

B. $O(n^2)$

C. $O(n\log_2 n)$

D. $O(n^3)$

> [!note]- 原题截图
> ![[relax1000DS 01 绪论 选择 015.png]]

> [!success]- 答案与解析
> **答案：** B
>
> **解析：**
>
> $$\sum_{i=\{1,3,5,\cdots,2p+1\}}\ \sum_{j=1,2,4,\cdots,2^q}\ \sum_{k=1}^{j}1$$
> $$=\sum_{i=\{1,3,5,\cdots,2p+1\}}\ \sum_{j=1,2,4,\cdots,2^q} j$$
> $$=\sum_{i=\{1,3,5,\cdots,2p+1\}} (1+2+4+\cdots+2^q)$$
> $$=\sum_{i=\{1,3,5,\cdots,2p+1\}} (2^{q+1}-1)\quad(2^q=n)$$
> $$=\sum_{i=\{1,3,5,\cdots,2p+1\}} (2n-1)$$
> $$\stackrel{p\approx n/2}{=} (2n-1)(n/2)=O(n^2)$$
